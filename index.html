Short Film · 2020

AKA

Dorothée Murail — Paris / Tokyo

Image

AKA responds to a rare demand: expressing through the mediating power of art an unspeakable state of being — a mysterious, pathological condition at the threshold between presence and disappearance.

The short film features a butoh dancer performing the "dance of the soul", described by master Kazuo Ohno as "a space that connects life and death." Shot on film, the work navigates the tension between honne — one's true inner feelings — and tatemae, the public mask one presents to the world.

The film exists in the space where identity begins to fracture: not yet broken, not yet whole. The butoh body becomes the site of that fracture — simultaneously present and disappearing, human and something else entirely.

Film Photography · 2021

Red Scale

Sankai Juku — Tokyo

Image

Red Scale was born and made in Japan, built around two concepts at the heart of Japanese social life: honne — the true self one keeps hidden — and tatemae — the face one presents to the world. Through the image, the series questions the duality between inner being and social facade.

The performers, Dai Matsuoka and Akihito Hichihara, dancers of butoh within the Sankai Juku company, embody this tension between interiority and representation. Their bodies carry both: the discipline of form and the rupture beneath it.

The redscale technique — film exposed in reverse — produces images that are degraded, burned, shifted toward red. It is chemical nostalgia: an image that has already begun to disappear before it exists.

Film Photography · 2020

In Between

Série argentique

Image

In Between is a photographic series exploring the interstices of the world — those floating spaces between places, moments and beings. Each image inscribes itself into a visual narrative where fragments accumulate to form a sensitive, poetic geography.

The human figures — faces never shown — carry no defined roles. They inhabit the image without dominating it: anonymous witnesses to a space held in suspension. They are humanity caught in its fleeting passage, inscribed in a world in perpetual motion.

The series was photographed across multiple countries, always in the same posture: waiting for the world to reveal its in-between states.

Curation · Tokyo · 2025

Shape of Light
Lin Syouki

KURA, Shibuya — May 2025

The first time I saw Lin's work, something became immediately clear: it wasn't an effect. It was the very principle of the piece. The gaze doesn't discover the image — it produces it.

Lin works reflective fabric as a living material: crumpling it, stretching it, fixing it. What results doesn't quite exist at a distance. You have to move to see it. You have to be there.

My intention was not to hang paintings, but to construct the conditions of an experience. Six years in Japan changed the way I compose space: emptiness is not an absence — it is a material.

Dorothée
Murail

An image-maker whose work explores thresholds — between cultures, between states, between what shows itself and what resists being seen.

Trained at La Cambre and Les Gobelins, she has developed over ten years a practice that crosses photography, cinema and visual research. Her personal projects — rooted in cultural tensions and interstitial spaces — have been presented at Centre Pompidou and Nuit Blanche Kyoto.

In parallel, she collaborates with leading houses and institutions — Cartier, Dior, Vogue — on projects where the image is at stake. Her curatorial work, born from a visual archive built over several years, extends the same posture: not to classify the world in images, but to reveal its frictions.
